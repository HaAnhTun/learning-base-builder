## word_frequency_en.tsv

Top 50,000 English words with Zipf frequency, exported once from
[wordfreq](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq) 3.1.1 (`top_n_list('en', 50000, wordlist='large')`,
`zipf_frequency(word, 'en', wordlist='large')`).

wordfreq code is Apache-2.0. The wordfreq data is redistributed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) license
and is built from sources including Google Books Ngrams, SUBTLEX (Brysbaert et al.), Wikipedia,
the Leeds Internet Corpus, ParaCrawl, Reddit and Twitter. This derived list is shared under the same license.

Robyn Speer. (2022). rspeer/wordfreq: v3.0 (v3.0.2). Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7199437
